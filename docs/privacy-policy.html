Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 - July - 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit or interact with our Site.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information when you use the Site:

  • Information you provide directly, such as your name and email address when you subscribe to a newsletter, leave a comment, or contact us through a form.
  • Automatically collected information, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, and time spent on the Site, gathered through cookies and analytics tools.
  • Cookies and tracking technologies, used to improve site functionality, understand visitor behavior, and remember your preferences.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Operate, maintain, and improve the Site
  • Send newsletters or updates, if you have subscribed
  • Respond to inquiries or comments submitted through the Site
  • Analyze site traffic and user behavior to improve content and user experience
  • Detect and prevent technical issues or fraudulent activity

Cookies

The Site uses cookies to enhance your browsing experience. Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help the Site remember your preferences and understand how visitors use the Site. You can disable cookies through your browser settings, though some features of the Site may not function properly without them.

Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services, such as analytics providers or advertising networks, that collect, monitor, and analyze information to help us understand Site usage. These third parties have their own privacy policies governing how they use the information they collect.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with trusted third-party service providers who assist in operating the Site, such as hosting providers or email delivery services, and only to the extent necessary for them to perform their services.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information we collect from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your data. To exercise these rights, please contact us using the information below.

Children's Privacy

The Site is not directed toward children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
